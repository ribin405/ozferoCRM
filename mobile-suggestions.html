Mobile status filters and menu

Three options for each, as live phone prototypes. Tap them: chips select, sheets open, and the drawer slides in. Sample data only.

1. Status filters on the enquiries list

Today the six chips wrap into three lines and push the list below the fold. These keep the filter to one line.

2. Main menu

Today the menu is a sideways-scrolling strip with Sign out hidden at the end. These put daily actions in reach of the thumb.

Recommended combination

  • Filters: option A. One swipeable row with counts. It is familiar, shows the active filter at a glance, and costs one line of height instead of three.
  • Menu: option A, the bottom tab bar. Dashboard, Enquiries, a center + to add an enquiry, Follow-ups, and a More sheet for Categories, Team, Settings, theme and Sign out. Add enquiry is the most frequent action and becomes one tap.
  • Admin-only items stay inside More with an Admin tag, so team members never see them.
